Join us for the most anticipated artisan market of 2026. The Lokal Indonesia Artisan Market returns with its biggest edition yet, celebrating Indonesian Independence Month with a curated showcase of the country's finest artisans, designers, and makers.

## What to Expect

Over three days, visitors will discover hundreds of unique handcrafted products, from intricate textiles and batik to ceramics, jewellery, woodwork, and artisan food. Every vendor has been personally curated by our team to ensure quality and authenticity.

## Program Highlights

- Artisan demonstrations and live crafting sessions
- Children's craft workshops
- Cultural performances throughout the weekend
- A dedicated food zone featuring local culinary artisans
- Exclusive limited-edition products from featured makers

## Getting There

Menara Sun Life is located in the Kuningan business district of South Jakarta, easily accessible by MRT (Rasuna Said station) and TransJakarta.

Parking is available on-site. We encourage visitors to use public transport to reduce congestion.
