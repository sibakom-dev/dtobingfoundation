Every year on Valentine's Day, we transform love into action. Our annual bake sale brings together volunteer bakers, sweet-toothed supporters, and the generous Jakarta community to raise funds for the children of Hati Suci Orphanage.

## Why It Matters

Hati Suci is one of the partner care homes we support through our Urip Gawe Urup program. The orphanage houses over 40 children and relies on community support for daily operations, education materials, and special activities.

## How It Works

Volunteer bakers donate their creations, and all sales proceeds go directly to Hati Suci. There are no operating costs to cover, so every rupiah you spend on a brownie or a slice of cake goes straight to the children.

## Get Involved

Want to bake for the cause? We are accepting donations of baked goods from the community. Please contact us to register as a volunteer baker.
