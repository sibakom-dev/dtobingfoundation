Lokal Indonesia is our curated artisan marketplace, a series of vibrant markets that bring Indonesia's most talented crafters directly to urban consumers who care about where their products come from.

Since our first market, we have hosted over 30 events across the archipelago, welcoming hundreds of artisan vendors and tens of thousands of visitors. Each market is a celebration, of Indonesian creativity, of community, and of the power of conscious commerce.

We carefully select vendors whose products represent authentic Indonesian craftsmanship, ensuring quality and provenance with every purchase. Alongside the markets, we provide vendors with business coaching, visual merchandising training, and community connections that extend far beyond market day.
